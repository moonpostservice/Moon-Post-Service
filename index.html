The World's Only Moon Postal Service

Not every messageshould arrive immediately.

Some words deserve distance. Anticipation. A sky.

Moon Post Service is the first moon-based messaging platform. A message can be sent only when the moon is above you.

It does not appear the moment you send it. It travels. It is released only when the moon rises where your recipient is.

That waiting is part of the feeling.

Because some messages should not arrive like every other notification. They should gather a little darkness around them. A little time. A little longing.

There was a time when sending something meant letting it go. A letter left your hands and crossed distance before it arrived. That distance gave it weight. Digital messaging erased much of that space between us.

Moon Post Service brings it back — not only as a form of nostalgia, but as a new way of tuning into the oldest rhythm above us.

Slowness deepens a feeling. So does knowing something is on its way.

And when two people share the same sky, Moon Post Service becomes something else entirely: a chat that wakes with the moon and falls silent when it sets.

And when the new moon comes, the messages return to the sky.

Messages live for one lunar cycle. Like moonlight, they're not meant to be stored — they're meant to be felt. A new cycle begins — fresh, dark, full of possibility.
