Basics

Moon Post Service is the world's only moon postal service.

You can send a message only when the moon is above you, and it arrives when the moon rises for the person you sent it to.

Moon Post Service opens at moonrise and closes at moonset. During that time, you can send and receive messages.

If you send a message to someone in another part of the world, it travels with the moon and arrives when the moon rises for them.

If you share the same sky, Moon Post Service becomes instant messaging for as long as the moon is above you both.

Messages live for one lunar cycle. At every new moon, all messages are deleted.

Yes.

Moon Post Service opens and closes according to moonrise and moonset at your location, even if clouds, buildings, or city lights hide the moon from view.

Why use it?

The moon becomes your daily ritual. When the moon appears, that is your moment to pause, look up, and check in on the people who matter to you.

Good things come slower. Moon messages do not arrive instantly. They travel with the moon, giving surprise, anticipation, and longing a little time to build.

Some relationships deserve a different place. Some conversations do not belong in the stream of updates, logistics, and digital noise. Some relationships deserve a different light, a different rhythm, and a different place.

A better signal for personal messages. Instead of another notification, the moon tells you when it is time to check in.

Terms, Privacy & Community Guidelines

Last updated: 29/5/2026

Moon Post Service is a free experimental project created to help humans connect with each other, and with the moon. It is not a professional communication service, not an emergency service, and not a guaranteed messaging platform. By using Moon Post Service, you agree to use it gently, responsibly, and at your own risk.

What to expect

We try our best to make Moon Post Service work beautifully. But you should not expect any guaranteed service, uptime, delivery, message storage, message recovery, lunar accuracy, technical support, or compensation of any kind.

Messages may be delayed, lost, unavailable, deleted, or undeliverable.

Do not use Moon Post Service for urgent, important, legal, medical, financial, safety-related, or time-sensitive communication.

Message lifetime

Messages are designed to live for one lunar cycle. At every new moon, messages are scheduled to disappear from active inboxes.

Some technical copies may remain temporarily in backups, logs, security systems, abuse reports, or where we need to keep them for safety, legal, or technical reasons.

Privacy and data

Moon Post Service may collect and process basic information needed to operate the service, such as account details, messages, approximate location or timezone, blocked users, reports, browser/device information, IP address, and technical logs.

We use this information to operate the service, calculate moon timing, deliver messages, maintain accounts, prevent abuse, improve the product, and protect users.

We do not sell your personal data.

The platform was built with the help of AI coding tools, including Claude Code. We did our best to build it responsibly and to protect the service and user data. Still, no online system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that data will always be protected from bugs, unauthorized access, technical failure, or loss.

Location

Moon Post Service uses location or approximate location to calculate when the moon is above you. We do not need your exact address, and we do not show your precise location to other users.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the main menu. Deleting your account removes or disables your active account, but some information may remain temporarily in backups, logs, abuse reports, or where needed for safety, security, technical, or legal reasons.

Moon Roulette

Moon Roulette is an optional feature that lets you send a moon message to another user selected by the service. You should not share sensitive personal information with strangers. You can delete the conversation, block the user, or report abuse from the message menu.

Community rules

Do not use Moon Post Service to harass, threaten, intimidate, shame, deceive, spam, stalk, scam, impersonate, or harm anyone.

Do not send sexual content to strangers.

Do not share another person's private information without permission.

Do not use Moon Post Service for illegal activity.

Do not try to contact someone who blocked you.

We may remove content, block users, suspend accounts, delete accounts, or report serious abuse if needed to protect users or operate the service.

Age

Moon Post Service is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old to use Moon Post Service.

Moon Roulette is intended for users who are at least 18 years old.

Contact

For questions, privacy requests, abuse reports, or safety concerns, contact the team through the app.
